Is it really a deal?

Every deal on this page was checked at its store on 27 Nov 2026. The badge on each card is our honest read:

  • NEW LOW — the lowest price any tracker has seen for this product.
  • STRONG — 30% off or more, or $100+ saved. A genuine bargain.
  • GOOD — 15–30% off. Worth buying if you wanted it anyway.
  • ROUTINE — under 15% off. A real cut, but not a reason to rush.

How we check
